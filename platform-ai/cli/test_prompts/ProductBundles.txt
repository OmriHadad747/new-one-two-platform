# ─────────────────────────── VERSION 1 — RICH ────────────────────────────
# Use this when you want to test how the pipeline handles a well-described merchant brief.

I'm selling on Shopify and want to start offering product bundles to lift my average order value.
Customers buy single products today; if I bundle related products with a discount I think I can
get them to add more to the cart. I should be able to create and manage bundles myself from the
Shopify admin — no developer help needed once it's set up.

Two bundle types matter. Fixed: I pick the exact products and the customer buys the whole pack
or nothing. Flexible: I define a pool and a count ("pick any 3 from this list") and the customer
chooses which items they want. Each bundle should let me pick products or specific variants.

The discount should be configurable per bundle. I want to choose between three discount kinds:
percentage off the bundle total, a flat amount off, or buy-X-get-Y-free where one of the items
is effectively included free. On top of that I want tiered discounts — the more items in the
bundle the bigger the cut. For example 2 items 10% off, 3 items 20% off, 4 items 30% off.

On the storefront the bundle UI should appear on the product page itself. The customer picks
their items (for flexible bundles), sees the discounted total live, and adds the whole bundle to
cart in one click — discount applied automatically at checkout. In the admin I want a table of
all my bundles, edit any field, enable/disable per bundle, and a count of times each was bought.

I want to sell product bundles to lift my AOV. From the admin I should be able to define
bundles — either fixed (specific products) or flexible (pick N from a pool). Each bundle has a
configurable discount: percentage, fixed amount, or buy-X-get-Y-free, with optional tiered
discounts (more items = bigger discount).

On the storefront the bundle shows on the product page; the customer picks their items, sees the
discounted price, and adds the whole bundle to cart in one click. I also want an admin table
listing all bundles with edit/enable/disable and purchase counts.
