Product bundling app for Shopify. Admin CRUD for bundles with fields: name, type (fixed or flexible), items list, discount kind (percent, fixed amount, or buy-X-get-Y-free), discount value, tiered discounts (item count to discount value), enabled flag. Resource picker for product/variant selection. Admin list view with edit, enable/disable, and purchase count per bundle. Storefront widget on the product page; for flexible bundles an item picker with min/max count; live discounted total; "Add bundle to cart" button adds the items and applies the discount code.
