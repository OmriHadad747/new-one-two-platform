App purpose: Customers earn points per order, see balance on cart page, and apply points as a discount before checkout.
App category: storefront_backend_admin

Quality brief:
A good version handles edge cases: preventing over-redemption (points can't exceed available balance), recalculating points correctly when orders are refunded, ensuring the discount appears before tax and shipping, and making the cart widget responsive on mobile. The admin settings should be simple — just two numbers (points per dollar and points-to-discount conversion). Show merchants actual customer point balances so they can audit the system.
